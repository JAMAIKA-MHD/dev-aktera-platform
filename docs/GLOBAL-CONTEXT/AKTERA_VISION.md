# AKTERA — Vision & Strategic Ambition

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** Strategic context

---

## 1. Company-level view

AKTERA is being built as a **marketing and customer-experience infrastructure company**.

The company is not fundamentally a gamification company.

The current product wedge is a Customer Engagement Platform that helps businesses create, deploy, operate, and learn from interactive customer experiences. Gamified campaigns are an important initial use case because they provide a concrete, demonstrable path to customer engagement, data collection, rewards, and measurement.

The broader ambition is deliberately larger than today's product scope.

AKTERA may expand into additional marketing and customer-experience niches as product understanding, technology, and market opportunities evolve.

---

## 2. Current product identity

**AKTERA = Customer Engagement Platform**

A platform for businesses to create and operate interactive customer experiences, collect useful consented customer data, manage rewards, and understand campaign performance.

The current product emphasizes interactive/gamified campaigns, but the identity should not be reduced to "games" or "gamification software."

---

## 3. Long-term strategic ambition

The long-term ambition is to build infrastructure that helps businesses create increasingly sophisticated customer experiences and marketing interactions.

Potential future areas include, but are not limited to:

- new interactive experience formats;
- new marketing and engagement workflows;
- richer visual experiences;
- computer-vision-related customer experiences;
- other emerging technologies that can create useful business-to-customer interactions.

These future areas are **vision/exploration**, not commitments to a specific product roadmap.

The strategic principle is to preserve enough optionality that AKTERA can move into adjacent high-value experience and marketing categories without being conceptually trapped by today's first wedge.

---

## 4. Vision statement

A useful internal formulation is:

> **Build the infrastructure through which businesses create, deploy, measure, and evolve meaningful customer experiences — starting with interactive engagement and expanding into broader forms of marketing and digital experience.**

This is an internal strategic formulation, not a fixed public slogan.

---

## 5. Strategic principles

### Customer engagement over isolated mechanics

The company should optimize for the customer relationship and business outcome, not for the novelty of a single game or interaction.

### Infrastructure over one-off campaigns

The product should become reusable infrastructure that supports many campaigns and experience types.

### Platform optionality

The present product architecture should not unnecessarily prevent future adjacent product categories.

### Local strength first

The initial product is designed around the Algerian market, its business environment, operational realities, and regulatory constraints.

### Build toward regional relevance

The broader vision includes the possibility of extending beyond Algeria into North Africa, the Middle East, and other appropriate markets when the product and business justify it.

### Learn through real usage

The product should evolve through demos, pilots, real campaigns, customer feedback, operational learning, and technical validation rather than relying only on assumptions.

---

## 6. What AKTERA must not become by accident

- A company whose identity is permanently limited to "games".
- A collection of isolated promotional microsites with no reusable platform value.
- A product architecture that treats today's MVP implementation choices as permanent constraints.
- A technology showcase that loses sight of customer/business outcomes.
